ORIGINAL: ## Why RSS Still Matters
TRANSLATED: ## 为什么 RSS 仍然重要
---
ORIGINAL: RSS gives readers control over what they follow, without an algorithm deciding what appears next.
TRANSLATED: RSS 让读者掌控自己关注的内容，而不是由算法决定接下来出现什么。
---
ORIGINAL: It also keeps subscriptions portable between applications.
TRANSLATED: 它还让订阅能够在不同应用之间迁移。
